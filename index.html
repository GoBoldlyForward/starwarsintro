After years of galactic silence, civilization is on the brink of more Star Wars movies. Now, with the Force already awakened, the people of Earth seek solace in creating websites, memes, and presentations that need the intro crawl we've come to know and love. Make cooler stuff, faster, with our simple CSS library for the Star Wars intro crawl.

Usage

Drop it in

One stylesheet, the markup pattern below, and a star-field background image (one is included at img/stars-bg.jpg).

<link rel="stylesheet" href="path/to/starwarsintro.css">

<div class="star-wars-intro">

  <p class="intro-text">A few days ago, during...</p>

  <h2 class="main-logo">
    <img src="img/your-logo.png">
  </h2>

  <div class="main-content">
    <div class="title-content">
      <p class="content-header">EPISODES IV-VI<br>A Movie Marathon</p>
      <p class="content-body">
        Your scrolling story here…
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

Notes

Pure CSS

No JavaScript, no other dependencies. The crawl is a CSS @keyframes animation over a perspective-projected stage. The whole library is one ~3 KB file.

The plugin sizes itself to fill its parent. On the demo above we scope it into a 75vh tall stage; in your page, place it inside any positioned element of your choice.
